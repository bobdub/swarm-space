## Scoped guidance
- Volcano effects sample baked crater terrain and shared-time wind; slopes use world-space mesh normals to keep light and ground aligned.
- Fish and shark fight pacing stays in the shared fishing engine, so the displayed bar and strike checks agree.
- Guest names live only on the guest identity, never shared avatar preferences; gate member routes, mining and post creation independently because guests retain P2P identities.
- Direct Brain guest entry runs only after session restoration resolves signed-out and before mounting the scene; deduplicate guest provisioning and recheck identity after key generation so returning or concurrently signed-in members are never overwritten.
- Session restoration rechecks the current identity before applying an asynchronous result, because guest entry or member login may have completed while the durable read was pending.
- The Global Cell Bus dials via `dialViaBus()` (backoff-respecting), never the manual `connectToPeer()` path; manual connect registers in the library and pulses presence before dialing — this keeps every flow on the working library pattern and prevents redial storms.
- Brain invites use the shared one-shot invite coordinator at signaling readiness before general discovery; already-running sessions use its guarded manual path, and chat status follows the named host connection rather than aggregate peer counts.
- Gate placement changes by maker identity; scope drafts by owner and room. Wall DOM occlusion ignores invisible targets and respects solid avatars.
- Read scoped subdirectory `AGENTS.md` before edits.
- Relay fallback is per-peer, only for stalled new dials, and never touches open connections (details in `src/lib/p2p/AGENTS.md`).
- Private land is claimed one grid block at a time with the Boundary Marker (`src/lib/world/boundaryMarker.ts`, held tool `tool_boundary_marker`): press and hold a block, sign the claim with the identity P-256 key before burning `BOX_PRICE_SWARM`, then record it in `landClaimsStore.ts`. Peers keep a claim only when `computeUserId(signerPublicKey) === ownerId` and it verifies; per block the earliest `claimedAt` wins (then owner id, then signature) so every peer agrees, and future claims (>5 min) are refused. `landPlots` folds block claims into rectangles for overlays and build permission. Claims on water (the village pond from `nature/pond.ts`, or any ocean or shore sample at a block's centre or corners, `isWaterBlock`) are refused locally and by every peer (`installLandClaimRules`). Every paid block is also a local deed (`brain-land-deeds-v1:<ownerId>`): when an earlier claim displaces it, the owner's device re-signs it on the nearest free non-water block (`reconcileDeeds`) at no charge. Own legacy walk-claimed plots become deeds with their original claim time (backup kept); dev roads (commons) stay local and are the only use of the walking survey.
- Targeted dials persist manual intent before readiness checks and never stack while a dial is in flight; room/Brain targets bus-dial as exchange without clearing backoff. Saved manual/library peers get a longer presence window (not unlimited), never bypassing cooldowns, pending-dial limits or disabled auto-connect, so friends survive brief gaps without redial storms against offline peers. Known peers (a real data channel opened before, stamped `lastConnectedAt`, or saved manual/library; 30-day horizon) are also rejoined without a fresh beacon: once when the node comes online, then with per-peer backoff (30 s doubling to 10 min), within the same cooldowns, budget and Auto-connect switch.
- Contact identity travels through active-peer library exchange; subscriptions invalidate discovered-peer snapshots independently of media, connected peers' own profiles override relayed hints, and Swarm panel edits use the live library without dialing so storage cannot diverge.
- Keep provider context identity in HMR data for Streaming, Onboarding, Walkthrough contexts.

- Sovereign recovery (`src/lib/backup/recoveryVault.ts`, `vaultCustody.ts`): the Recovery Link is also shown as a QR code in Settings → Security and names one vault holder (`chooseRecoveryHolder`: the person's pick, else a connected custodian, else any custodian, else this node); `/recover` can scan it (`RecoveryQrScanner`, BarcodeDetector or a photo, read locally) and parses pasted or scanned text with `recoverySearchFromLink`. No new dependency is needed for scanning. Otherwise: the local account password only wraps the on-device key and never leaves the browser; the mesh holds only the outer-shell vault (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA-256 250k, random salt, GCM AAD binds userId+version) signed by the identity key. Peers hold an envelope only when `computeUserId(signerPublicKey) === userId` and its version is strictly higher, capped at 256 held vaults. Genesis v1 is sealed with the signup password (`genesisDefault`) and the Security panel nudges rotation; rotated phrases must differ from the account password. Never export raw private keys.
- Account merge (`src/lib/p2p/mergeProtocol.ts`, `src/lib/blockchain/accountMerge.ts`, Settings → Security → Merge Account): pure pull, so an incoming `merge:offer` is only held in memory and shown when the panel is open (it probes connected peers on open); never toast, notify or sound. The 6-digit code (3-minute TTL, 3 attempts) never crosses the wire: each side proves it with an HMAC bound to session, identity and nonce. Both devices unlock with their local password before Execute. Guests have no password or vault and cannot merge: the wizard asks them to create an account, their executor has no merge identity, and a summary marked `guest` ends the session. The retiring key tips every spendable asset to the master with ordinary signed ledger transfers (SWARM/MINTME and every other tippable asset from `planAssetSweep`, such as imported tokens like Dreams, deposited ETH or creator tokens, as `wallet_tip`; one credit transfer); an imported deposit still waiting on peer sync blocks the merge before anything moves, the bundle carries the imported token definitions (master adds them to its wallet) and their custody sync records (sent as `merge:custody-part`, re-verified on the master), and untippable assets (sub-chains, unbridged records) stay and are named in the wizard and sends on-chain MINTME for real first (a failure blocks the merge before anything moves), signs `account_merged`, exports posts/favorites/contacts, and signs its `account_tombstone` only after the master's signed ack verifies, so a dropped link never half-retires an account. The master acks only once every listed transfer is recorded on its ledger (a retry re-lists earlier merge transfers); the MintMe address is always read on-chain (unconfirmed arrivals block, unsynced ones are synced first), and accounts above `MAX_MERGE_POSTS` are refused before anything moves. The master re-attributes posts (same id, `mergedFrom` = attestation) and records aliases at `account:aliases:<masterId>`. A live account tombstone retires that identity's recovery vault everywhere (`setVaultRetirementCheck`, `onAccountRetired`). "Sync to Master" installs the master only through its encrypted recovery vault and passphrase; raw keys never travel.

- Bar drinks (`src/lib/blockchain/infinity/barTab.ts`, `src/lib/pub/barOrders.ts`, `BarMenuPanel`): ordering at the counter is a signed `pool_donate` into the Market Vault whose signed meta carries `barTab` (drink id, name, qty). It funds the vault like any donation but is the bar tab's record only: `donationFromTx` must keep skipping `isBarTabTx`, and the tab never counts plain donations.

## Brain chat participation
- Keep guest participation preferences separate from member preferences; disconnected guest guidance is local interface status and uses the existing manual connection path, not fabricated Infinity output.
- Keep Infinity participation as an explicit saved mode separate from temporary voice mute; gate generation and delayed replies with current mode and invalidate queued replies on disable/unmount so silence is immediate.
- Merge partial hub preferences. Only the opted-in prompt originator generates a correlated room reply; receivers deduplicate and ask off viewers once per room — shared chat must not diverge into private replies.
- Chat and media wrapping share ledger-backed coin recovery; count historical containers and balance so spent coins cannot be recreated.
- Infinity and Brain world rules (learning, memory, Earth, terrain, sky, mute, room membership) are in `src/lib/brain/AGENTS.md`.
- Brain atmosphere and star visibility share `skyCadenceAt` from the current Earth pose and camera-local solar horizon; celestial directions stay frame-rate independent so night agrees across devices without a second clock.

## Wallet boundaries
- Vault receipts use payout legs; pool funding stays ledger-derived to avoid double counting.
- Keep the cold-wallet guide in a dedicated lazy-loaded public page linked from Settings and Wallet; documentation must distinguish external custody, local ledger records and peer sync rather than promise consensus.
- Keep native receiving addresses visible at the wallet entry and route native transfers through the signed wallet-tip ledger, independently of MetaMask; external export stays separate because an external address is not native settlement.
- Resolve native wallet aliases via the shared async address resolver and race-safe display hook; never migrate balances or credit history to change displayed addresses — aliases are not new ownership.
- Route tips by explicit asset identity, preserve credit economics, and block unsupported external transfers rather than simulating delivery.
- SWARM tips sign their engraving weight (meta tipFormat 2 + weight, src/lib/blockchain/wallets/tipWeight.ts); Hollow is the default and the only choice without held engraved coins, legacy unweighted tips read as Hollow, and the weight labels the payment without moving coin custody.
- Native SWARM tips require identity-bound P-256 signatures and ledger balance checks before acceptance; sub-chain tips stay blocked until verifiable asset settlement exists.
- Creator-token tips (`src/lib/blockchain/wallets/creatorTokenTips.ts`) settle on a signed per-token ledger: the `wallet_tip` names the token (`tokenId`, asset `creator:<tokenId>`), carries exact 6-decimal `amountUnits`, and signs the sender's `openingUnits`, their market-issued holding apart from tips (at most the max supply; only the highest opening a holder signed counts, so it never falls). Peers accept a tip only when `opening + received − sent` covers it, so tokens are conserved and never spent twice, and tokens received by tip are proven by the tips that delivered them; the opening itself is attested by the holder, not proven, until market issuance is signed on the ledger. The tippable balance is the smaller of the local holding and that ledger figure. Accepted tips move the signed-in member's holdings once each (`applyCreatorTokenTips`, applied ids kept on the holding as `ledgerTips`; `reconcileCreatorTokenTips` catches tips that arrived inside synced blocks). Tips never change circulating supply or move SWARM.
- Native wallet screens use direct MintMe reads and cold-key sends; preserve historical MetaMask records and unrelated external donation/RPC integrations because receiving requires no external wallet session.
- Run configured src tests with Vitest (bun run test; test:wallet for wallet regressions); use isolated mocks and deterministic public wallet fixtures because Bun test APIs and random ethers entropy conflict with the jsdom runner.
- Synchronize the gateway from an app-wide verified external-wallet session, not a route panel or cached address, so navigation cannot misrepresent connection state.
- Gate external SWARM install/switching on a fresh HTTPS JSON-RPC chain-ID preflight; share UI readiness via a cached query, never the local provider — MetaMask must reach its own RPC.
- Host public mesh RPC outside this client-only repo; it requires ledger/mesh access.
- Build companions outside the client tree: loopback-only, authenticated pairing, allowlisted reads, never raw-transaction settlement.
- Local installs need fresh loopback JSON-RPC preflight; retain HTTPS validation — local helpers are not public gateways.
- Wallet history is a read-only merge (`src/lib/history/unifiedHistory.ts`) of the Credits store and the SWARM chain; each entry keeps its asset and settlement, credit_sync mirrors and duplicate wrap debits are folded in the view only, and balances are never derived from it. Show it as one card on the Ledger tab only; Labor keeps no separate Credits list. Rows read Asset | Action | Direction | Weight-type | Amount: only SWARM carries engraving weight (the named coin's seal tier, else Hollow); Credits are Minted, imported tokens Ported, SWARM-created tokens CREATOR.
- Read `src/lib/blockchain/AGENTS.md` for vault/key rules. Native Assets and Market share positive-balance inventory; unsupported payments fail closed.
- Persist public cold-key receiving metadata at identity unlock, independently of the tab-session signing seal, so receiving never requires a withdrawal key when the address is known.
- Room signaling relays directed media signals and room announcements at most 2 hops with dedup keys, because peers in one Brain room may share no direct mesh edge.
- Each new mesh link re-hellos joined rooms (relayed), since a direct Brain entry announces before any link exists. The loading chain runs essential points (local, mesh) first and polls the QScore while paused; the guardrails bus only reports level changes.
- Weighted (Creator Proof) coins are identified only by the explicit creatorProof tag stamped in applySeal (src/lib/blockchain/weightedCoin.ts); legacy coins are never inferred weighted, so every seal path reaches one flag.
- Content lifecycle (`src/lib/p2p/contentLifecycle.ts`): post/account tombstones are signed with the identity P-256 key and accepted only when `computeUserId(signerPublicKey) === authorId` (the device Ed25519 post key is not author-bound); post tombstones are keyed by author+post id, kept 30 days, refuse resurrected content and are answered back to the sender. Fadeout uses author last-seen from authored timestamps, live profile exchange and max-merged peer hints, never relay time; own posts and device-local favorites (`postFavorites`, never a post field) never fade, tombstones override favorites, and purges keep coin-anchored or own files.
- Posts/comments load into coins by flipping `_coinId` on the content itself (`src/lib/blockchain/contentPayload.ts`); unflipped content (incl. legacy) waits for the next hollow coin, so posting never depends on mined SWARM and nothing is loaded twice.
- MintMe mining rules are in `src/lib/blockchain/AGENTS.md`.
- "My Content Only" (dashboard switch, `feed.myContentOnly`, OFF by default so peer content syncs and shows naturally; the legacy `feed.showNetworkContent` key is ignored) shows strictly the local identity's own posts via `isOwnPost` (`src/lib/content/ownContent.ts`: user id, identity public key, merge aliases, or `_origin: 'local'` signed by this device's Ed25519 key); walled network posts get no exception.
- Trading Stand (`src/lib/world/tradingStand/`, prefab `trading_stand_wood`): pricing is pure (`pricing.ts`) and reuses the Market Vault rules from `marketVaultPricing.ts`: a 40% protected floor of the stand vault is never spent, quotes use the 60% liquid layer, one trade takes at most 10% of that layer or of a good's stock, and stock prices are asymptotic (`base·K/(stock+K)`, a buy takes at most 10% of stock) so a good is never bought out to zero. Credits quotes are the SWARM quote times the live `spotCreditsPerSwarm`, never a fixed 100:1. Stand vault state (`tradingStandStore.ts`) is ledger-derived from mined blocks like the Market Vault, with payouts also checked against this device's unmined entries; goods legs are `custom_token_transfer` in `GOOD:<id>` to/from the stand vault. Every placed stand has its own vault (`standVaultScope`, `trading-stand:stand:<placementId>`) holding its stock, SWARM and Credits; stand trades and funding never touch the main Market Vault (the room/Cell `standScopeFor` scopes remain only so early stand records still read). Goods move only through `spendHarvested`/`spendMaterials` and `recordHarvestForResource` (honey, eggs, chickens, fish, sharks, gems, dirt, clay, gold and salt are `good:` bag keys, kept out of element lists and shown in the Gathered tally via `gatheredGoodTotals`; the stand sells the Food material as Fruit); SWARM and Credits move through the Market's transfer paths; any other payment fails closed. The stand opens from a 3.5 m proximity prompt; its sign shows the builder's name as in-scene 3D text (`SafeText`, not a DOM overlay): `PlacementRecord.ownerName` (display only, stamped at placement; ownership stays `actorId`), else the builder's profile name from the local `users` store (`useBuilderName`). Each stand is its own market: every sale splits like a creator market buy (`splitStandPayment`, the 40/40/15/5 `CREATOR_VAULT_*_SHARE` constants): 80% (buyback and stability) into the stand's vault, 15% to its owner (`actorId`), 5% to the community pool (SWARM as a pool-inflow `vault-sell` transfer; Credits into the Market's Credits reserve). Owner and community legs are paid before the vault leg; the owner buying at their own stand pays the full price and the vault pays their 15% back. Trades refuse any scope that is not a stand's own vault. Only the owner funds or stocks a stand, into its own vault: `donateToStandVault` (SWARM, Credits or synced tokens; anything else fails closed) and `donateGoodsToStand` (goods) both refuse anyone but the stand's `actorId`, and the modal shows the stock and funding controls to the owner only. The modal reads the stand and its owner from the stand itself (`resolveStand`: the placement record, else the placed block's `placedBy`) and always trades on `standVaultScope(placementId)`; it never falls back to the viewer's room or Cell market. A stand trade is on the chain the moment it is made: its legs wait in this device's pending pool (persisted) until a block takes them. Prices quote from mined stock (`sharedStandVaultState`), while the stand's Stock column shows this device's ledger (`localStandStock`, mined plus pending) and marks the difference "awaiting block", so a sale stays visible across reloads. The owner's Credits 15% is also recorded on the chain (`tradeLeg: 'owner-cut'`). The Wallet's Labor tab lists every stand trade (`standLedgerFor`, one row per `operationId`) with its mined status.
- Offline gathers stay local (`src/lib/world/playMode.ts`, `harvestedInventory.ts`): play is online only while P2P is enabled with at least one open peer link (`P2PProvider` → `setMeshOnline`), unknown counts as offline. A gather in offline play lands in the offline bag (`offline:<symbol>` keys) and earns no labour or engraving; offline play sees and spends it first, online play cannot see it. Anything that reaches the ledger or a coin (Trading Stand trades and stock via `tradingStand/goods.ts`, coin crafting) reads and spends with `{ onlineOnly: true }`, and ledger-backed receipts and their refunds record with origin `'online'`. The one exception is `offlineProjectSeed.ts`: a project's owner, while the project has no other members, pays `PROJECT_SEED_FEE_SWARM` (10, a `vault-sell` pool inflow with `meta.projectSeed`) before the offline bag moves into that project's seed pool (`seed:<projectId>|<symbol>`), which is usable only in that project's Brain (`setActiveBrainProject`) and never by an online-only read.
- Digging and beach goods (`toolActions.ts`): each successful dig step yields one good by the shell it cut (`digYieldForShell`): grass, soil and dirt give Dirt, mixed earth gives Clay, the Gold Vein gives Gold; bedrock and the seams give nothing. Gold takes the Prospector's Pick (`tool_pick_prospector`, the only tool with `oreVeins`): `goldToolRefusal` turns every other tool away before any swing, and in `applyImpact` an `oreVeins` tool works an ore vein at its host-rock `EarthShell.workDensity` (2.65) while every other tool strikes the full density (gold 19.3), so a Stone Pick cannot cut gold. The Prospector's Pick is bought, not built (`priceSwarm: 30` on the prefab, `src/lib/world/toolPurchases.ts`): 30 SWARM, or the same value in Credits at the live `spotCreditsPerSwarm` (falling back to `CREDIT_TO_SWARM_RATIO`), paid into the Market Vault (SWARM as a `vault-sell` pool inflow, Credits into the Market Credits reserve); ownership is read from the ledger by `meta.toolPurchase`. Beach and water checks sample `sampleSurfaceClass` in the Earth-local frame (`worldPosToLocalNormal`), the same frame the target picker uses. `DIG_MAX_DEPTH_M` is the bottom of the Gold Vein, so the vein is reachable but nothing below it. A shovel on the shore (`surfaceClass` 'shore', the beach band) gathers Salt; a bucket there still takes water. A prefab with `goodCost` costs gathered goods instead of its derived materials (the Salt Rock costs 1 Salt), and `checkAffordable`/`spendMaterials` read and draw those from their `good:` keys.
- Stop Support (`src/lib/content/unsupportedPostsStore.ts`) is a device-level opt-out of one post: its own IndexedDB database plus a localStorage mirror for synchronous reads. While listed, feeds and PostCard drop the post, `shouldAcceptIncomingPost` refuses copies, the mesh neither relays the post nor serves its manifests/chunks and deregisters this node as a seeder, and walls pinning it are not materialized (no block, collider or billboard). The local copy is kept so Resume Support (Node Dashboard → Unsupported Content) restores it without a refetch.
