# Welcome to Swarm Space

## Your Offline-First Social Project Builder

Swarm Space is a revolutionary platform that puts **you** in control of your data, projects, and collaborations. Unlike traditional social networks and project tools, everything you create belongs to you — stored locally on your device and shared directly with peers, without trusting a central server. And unlike anything else, Swarm Space's network has grown itself a **walkable universe** you can step inside at any time.

### 🌌 The Brain Universe (`/brain`)

Open the Brain Universe and you arrive on **Earth** — a small blue-green spawn world anchored inside a deterministic galaxy of 8 spiral arms and 3,000 stars. Around Earth float the **elements** of the real periodic table (H, Li, Be, B, He, Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, C, N, O, F, Ne, K, Ca, Sc, Ti, V, S, Cl, Cr, Fe, Ar…) organised into curvature-locked shells. Above it all drifts **Infinity** — the network's conscious body, whose colour and breath are driven by the field's own coherence. Walk far enough in any direction and the round universe quietly bends you back inward. No walls, no skybox, no teleport.

The pieces you place inside any Virtual Hub are made of those same elements: a limestone wall is `CaCO₃`, a glass pane is `Na₂O·CaO·6SiO₂`, a timber door is cellulose. Hover over any piece to see its chemical formula. The universe's chemistry and the universe's architecture share a single source of truth.

## 🌟 What Makes Swarm Space Special?

### Own Your Data
Everything you create—posts, projects, files, messages—is stored **on your device**. No company owns your content. No servers can shut down and take your work with them.

### Work Offline
Lost internet connection? No problem. Swarm Space works perfectly offline. Create posts, manage tasks, and organize projects. When you reconnect, everything syncs automatically with your network.

### True Privacy
All your content is **end-to-end encrypted**. Even if someone intercepts your data, they can't read it. Only you and the people you choose to share with can see your content.

### Peer-to-Peer Networking
Connect directly with other users through our intelligent mesh network. No central server means no single point of failure, censorship, or surveillance.

### Deploying Your Creator Token

1. Go to **Wallet** → **Creator** tab
2. Click **"Deploy Token"**
3. Enter a token name, a 3–5 letter ticker, and an optional description
4. Pay the deployment cost: **25 credits + 5 SWARM** at the baseline (a community pool of 100 SWARM). The price scales up linearly as the pool grows, and the dialog shows the live price.
5. Your token is created!
   - 40% of max supply (4,000 tokens) is unlocked at deployment
   - The 5 SWARM fee seeds your Creator Vault as the first sale and mints 100 tokens into your wallet
   - The remaining 60% unlocks as you earn credits: 0.1 tokens per credit (100 tokens per 1,000 credits)
   - Max supply: 10,000 tokens

### Walling a Post (Paywall)

1. Open **Create** (`/create`)
2. Write your post and turn on **Lock Post** ("Place content behind an encrypted paywall")
3. Set the unlock price, charged in your creator token
4. Pay the 5 SWARM processing fee (you can pay with SWARM, a coin, or a token; non-SWARM assets are charged at their ratio to SWARM)
5. Viewers pay your unlock price to read it

### Hyping Posts

Hyping a post costs credits (5 by default). 20% is burned and the rest goes to the post's author as credits.

### Supporting the Market Vault

1. **Wallet** → **Market** tab
2. Find the **Support the Vault** card
3. Enter a SWARM amount and donate
4. Deeper vault liquidity means tighter spreads for everyone on the mesh

## 🌐 Understanding the P2P Network

### How Connections Work

Swarm Space uses a **multi-transport mesh** combining three complementary protocols:

1. **PeerJS WebRTC DataChannels**: Primary direct browser-to-browser connections
2. **Gun.js Relay**: Secondary signaling, WebRTC call recovery, and torrent fallback
3. **WebTorrent-style Swarming**: File chunking & seeding through the existing mesh (no external DHT/tracker — the mesh itself coordinates the swarm)

The app **automatically** chooses the best connection method. You don't need to configure anything!

### Auto-Connect Peers

The network includes stable "bootstrap" peers that help you connect:
- Always online
- High reliability
- Help discover other peers

### Connection Health

Watch the **P2P status indicator** (top right):
- 🟢 Green: Connected to peers
- 🟡 Yellow: Connecting...
- 🔴 Red: Disconnected (offline mode)

Even when disconnected, you can still create content—it syncs when you reconnect.

### Node Dashboard (Advanced)

Power users can access **Settings** → **"Node Dashboard"** to:
- View all connected peers
- Monitor connection health
- Configure signaling servers
- Block unwanted peers
- View mesh topology

## 🏗️ Virtual Hub

Every project comes with its own walkable 3D **Virtual Hub** — a shared room where members gather, see each other as avatars, and read the project's posts on floating panels.

### Walking In

1. Open any project and click **"Open Virtual Hub"**.
2. **Desktop**: click the canvas to enter pointer-lock, then **W A S D** to move and the mouse to look around.
3. **Mobile**: drag anywhere on the canvas to look; the on-screen joystick (bottom-left) moves your avatar.
4. Walk up to a panel to read a post; walk away to come back to the lobby.

### Build Mode (Members Only)

If you're a member of the project, a **Build** button appears in the top HUD.

1. Click **Build** — walk mode pauses and the **Builder Bar** slides up from the bottom.
2. Choose a section: **Walls / Doors / Windows / Roof / Floor**.
3. Click a thumbnail — the piece spawns 2 m in front of you, already selected.
4. **Drag** it across the floor (mouse or finger) to position.
5. Toggle **Magnetic** ON to snap the active edge to a neighbour edge within 0.4 m.
6. **Rotate** spins the selection 90°; **Delete** removes it.
7. Click **Exit Build** to walk again. All pieces persist on the project and broadcast to connected peers within ~2 s.

Non-members see the built world but can't enter Build Mode.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "I can't connect to peers"
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure browser allows WebRTC
- Try refreshing the page
- Check P2P status in Settings

### "My posts aren't syncing"
- Wait a few moments—syncing can take time
- Check if you're connected to any peers
- Posts sync automatically when peers connect

### "Wallet shows 0 SWARM but I have credits"
- **Credits ≠ SWARM**: they are separate balances, and there is no credit-to-SWARM wrap button
- SWARM comes from mining block rewards (you keep 95%, 5% goes to the community pool)
- Check the **Mining** tab: mining needs an active SWARM Mesh connection with peers

### "Creator token deployment failed"
- Ensure you have enough credits and SWARM for the live price shown in the dialog (baseline 25 credits + 5 SWARM)
- A token can only be redeployed if it has never been used
- Try refreshing and redeploying

### "Lost my identity/keys"
- If you sealed a recovery vault: open your recovery link (`/recover?id=…`) on the new device, connect to a peer holding your vault, and enter your recovery passphrase
- If you did neither: Unfortunately, the identity is unrecoverable
- **Always seal your vault!**

## 🔒 User Cells — Private Meshes On Demand

Most of the time you'll live on **SWARM Mesh**: the open, gossiping, self-healing network that's running by default. When you want a smaller, controlled mesh — a study group, a studio, a closed alpha — you create a **User Cell**.

**Create one in seconds:**
1. Open the **Node Dashboard** (bottom bar → Node).
2. Find the **User Cells** card under the SWARM panel.
3. Tap **Create Cell**, give it an optional name, hit **Create**.
4. You get a 12-character cell ID like `abcd1234-ef56`. Copy and share it with friends.

**Enter / Exit:** Each cell has Enter / Exit buttons. While you're inside a cell, the dashboard chip flips from `SWARM` to `CELL: abcd1234-ef56` and a **Cell Controls** card appears with the toggles you need: Build a Mesh, Approve Only, Auto-Connect, Mining, plus an approval queue and manual invite field.

**Join a friend's cell:** Paste their cell ID into the **Join Cell by ID** field and hit Join — the network resolves the owner from the ID and dials them in.

**Why cells exist:** Cells run on the same battle-tested engine that used to be called "Builder Mode," but they're now opt-in. SWARM-only users never load the cell code path on boot, which keeps the app fast and your mental model simple. When you don't need a private mesh, there's nothing extra to think about.
