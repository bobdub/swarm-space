
- Two-way knock: manual/invite dials send a 30s-TTL deduplicated invite-knock over the Global Cell Gun registry; recipients dial back via dialViaBus so carrier-NAT pairs punch simultaneously, with community TURN as last-resort fallback after STUN.
- Invite links are parsed by `resolveInviteLocation` (path, hash-routed and `?peer=` forms) and canonicalized in `main.tsx` before the router mounts. Manual dials during `connecting`/`reconnecting` queue (60s TTL) and flush on online; knocks queue until Gun is ready. Signaling endpoints come from `signalingEndpoints.ts` in one shared order (configured VITE_PEERJS_* first, PeerJS Cloud last) because servers do not share peer registries.
