
- Two-way knock: manual/invite dials send a 30s-TTL deduplicated invite-knock over the Global Cell Gun registry; recipients dial back via dialViaBus so carrier-NAT pairs punch simultaneously, with community TURN as last-resort fallback after STUN.
- Invite links are parsed by `resolveInviteLocation` (path, hash-routed and `?peer=` forms) and canonicalized in `main.tsx` before the router mounts. Manual dials during `connecting`/`reconnecting` queue (60s TTL) and flush on online; knocks queue until Gun is ready and use their own registry slot (`inviteKnockSlot`) so presence beacons cannot overwrite them. Signaling endpoints come from `signalingEndpoints.ts` in one shared order (configured VITE_PEERJS_* first, PeerJS Cloud last) because servers do not share peer registries.
- The heartbeat sweep never evicts a quiet peer whose data channel and connection are still open until 5 minutes (throttled background tabs heartbeat about once a minute; ICE consent closes dead links itself). A dropped live link stamps the library entry as seen and redials in seconds through `autoConnectLibrary`, so discovered peers are not left for the 2-minute cell retry.
- Simultaneous dials resolve to one agreed channel: when a second channel to the same peer opens, both sides keep the one opened by the lower peer ID (`replacesDuplicateChannel`, outgoing channels tracked in `outgoingChannels`), so neither closes the channel the other kept.
- A signaling socket drop on a live Peer reconnects in place (`recoverSignaling`: `peer.reconnect()` with the same ID, up to 4 tries) and keeps data channels and phase `online`; automatic dials are skipped and manual dials queue while `peer.disconnected`, flushing on recovery. Only a destroyed Peer or a socket that never returns takes the full rebuild. After the fast reconnects run out the node stays enabled in `failed` and retries every 2 minutes and on the window `online` event; it never disables itself.
- The Auto-connect toggle governs every automatic dial path, including the startup cascade and the Global Cell Bus cycle (`isAutoConnectEnabled`); explicit invites, knocks and room targets still dial. Turning it on cascades immediately.
- App-level room/media meetings use `registerPeerConnection` before readiness checks; actual data opens share the existing library protocol. Track reciprocal sends per channel with a WeakSet and never rebroadcast timestamp-only refreshes, so reconnections handshake without gossip loops or bypassing dial policy.
- Known-peer rejoin (`rejoinKnownPeers`): presence beacons are not required to reach someone you have really connected to. Channel open stamps `lastConnectedAt`; on going online, each reconnect-loop tick and signaling recovery, known peers within `KNOWN_PEER_REJOIN_HORIZON_MS` are dialed with per-peer backoff (`KNOWN_PEER_REJOIN_BACKOFF_MS`, reset on open). Gossip-learned strangers never count as known, however fresh. Legacy entries count as known when saved manual/library or when connection-affinity history shows time together.
- Peer-announced torrents enter a single-flight FIFO (`enqueueDownload`, state `queued`); the slot frees in `releaseDownloadSlot` on verified completion, hash error, any pause (manual, stall, bloat), dead-seed removal or `remove`, so every exit path must call it. Nothing bypasses the queue: `download()`, `resume()` and the hourly bloat retry enqueue with `{ next: true }` (front of the line, still behind the active download); only `pumpDownloadQueue` may call the private `startDownload`. Post attachments are capped by `MESH_POST_UPLOAD_LIMIT_BYTES` (`src/lib/media/meshUploadLimits.ts`), and `FileUpload` encrypts one file at a time across drops. Audio over the cap is not rejected: `src/lib/media/audioTranscode.ts` re-encodes it in the browser (mediabunny/WebCodecs, Opus then AAC, `.m4a`) at the highest bitrate that fits the cap for its duration, 24–128 kbps, before it is chunked.
- Torrents yield to calls. `torrentSwarm.standalone.ts` stays import-free: the mesh adapter supplies `isCallActive()` (from `callActivity.ts`, a probe the WebRTC manager registers) and `getBufferedAmount()` (data-channel + PeerJS backlog). While a call is live, downloads use at most 2 peers with 1 piece in flight each, and pieces are served to at most 2 peers at a time. Outgoing pieces always wait in a low-priority outbox until the peer's backlog is under 256 KB (64 KB in a call). Call senders are marked `priority`/`networkPriority: 'high'` on connect and after each stable negotiation.
- Post media pulls (`ensureManifestAndChunks` in `swarmMesh.standalone.ts`) are single-flight FIFO across every caller; duplicate asks share one promise, `{ next: true }` (media being opened) jumps to the front without interrupting, and a file with two consecutive unservable chunks yields its turn to the retry timer. The dashboard shows MOVING / QUEUED / WAITING from `getAssetPullState`.
- Build handoff (`appVersion.ts`): channel-open build stamps are hints only; verify a newer deployment through the tab's own no-store `version.json`, at most once daily per device. Never auto-refresh: only explicit Update App reloads. `appUpdateNotices.ts` tracks actual Brain mounts rather than URLs; outside Brain, record one identity/deployment inbox notice, while Brain presents a local namespace-addressed system line excluded from learning, broadcast and coin history. Resolve namespaces from public cold receiving metadata, never signing keys, so updates cannot interrupt calls or gameplay.
- Disabling the mesh says goodbye first: `stop()` runs `onBeforeStop` handlers (the signaling bridge broadcasts `leave-room` for its rooms and `chat-here {open:false}`, then lets go of everyone locally without recording departures) and sends `{type:'goodbye'}` on every open link, destroying the Peer `GOODBYE_FLUSH_MS` later. A received goodbye drops that link without a redial (2-minute redial pause, lifted by a fresh beacon or their own dial) and fires `onPeerDeparted`; the bridge then evicts the peer from rooms (as left), the call and the network chat. Any peer silent (no heartbeat or message) for `PEER_GONE_AFTER_SILENCE_MS` (5 min) is departed the same way, link or not. A fresh join brings them back.
